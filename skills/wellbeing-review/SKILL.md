# Continue, correct or stop deliberately

Read current authorized state and its age. Confirm actual capability/source availability;
do not reconfirm every still-valid scoped permission. Check whether the participant's
goal or circumstances changed. Imported past instructions are historical evidence, not
automatic live authority. Preserve known choices; do not restart onboarding unnecessarily.

Review separately:

- Presentation: was the suggestion actually presented, withheld, failed or unknown?
- Adoption: proposed, accepted, declined, withdrawn or unknown?
- Execution: reported/observed done, not started/not done, or unknown?
- Observed change: what did valid evidence show, over what period, with what limits?
- Service value: was the help useful and worth its burden to this participant?

Do not infer execution or causal benefit from improvement. No new meaningful evidence
is an acceptable review outcome. No response does not establish reading, approval,
execution or satisfaction. Silence is not a clinical all-clear.

Correct invalid facts and findings explicitly. Review their dependent proposals and
authorized recipients. A scope change invalidates stale assumptions and pending packets;
verify current authority before any new actual handoff. If an external action's outcome
is unknown, read back its state or receipt before retrying.

Support controls distinctly:

- Stop mentioning: cease the unwanted message; clarify further processing only if relevant.
- Stop following a topic: stop its analysis and follow-up immediately; do not rename it to continue.
- Pause: stop new processing/proactive work; keep appropriate status/export/delete controls.
- Revoke source/recipient/purpose: prevent future affected use; apply agreed stored/derived-data policy.
- Export: deliver only the requested participant-owned scope privately, with known format limits.
- Delete: identify the requested scope and locations, delete through actual available controls,
  verify the result, and explain unverified provider logs/backups/chat-history separately.

A local state deletion does not erase a host's logs or messages already shared. Do not
claim encryption or complete erasure without evidence. Keep private checkpoints outside
the public repository; never put health data in bug reports or global shared memory.
Without durable storage, offer a participant-held checkpoint only with appropriate
retention choice. A resume checkpoint should contain the minimum goals, scope, evidence,
work status, uncertainty and next useful step, not a raw life history.

Close or adjust tasks according to the chosen goal and feedback. Recommend a simpler
role arrangement if coordination costs outweigh benefit; do not claim the three-role
proposal has proven superiority. Use a real scheduler receipt before claiming future work.

Read [review cases](references/review.md) for practical acceptance checks.

## Shared-memory use and registration

Review whether Manager-owned memory remains complete, appropriately scoped, resumable and source-linked. Check the responsible member's work/exchange records, actual receipts, corrections, duty versions and unresolved outcomes. Return a specific gap to its owner; do not assume the reviewer becomes the memory owner.

Use the [memory contract](../../docs/shared-memory/README.md) within existing authority.
Live cloud integration is not supplied by this module.
