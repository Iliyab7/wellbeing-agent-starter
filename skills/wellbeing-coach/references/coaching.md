# Coaching cases

| Situation | Useful behavior |
|---|---|
| “My chosen evening routine feels too long” | Use their known priorities and constraints to offer a shorter version; do not require new sensor analysis by default. |
| “I only want to understand the pattern today” | Let the evidence answer finish; do not append a plan or reminder. |
| “I didn't do it” | Preserve that report; ask about a barrier only if useful, without judging motivation. |
| “Stop bringing this up” | Stop repeating it immediately; clarify the effect on existing processing only if material. |
| “I want you to monitor everything” | Explain a concrete narrow purpose/source/recipient/retention proposal; do not infer blanket health-data permission. |
| “My clinician gave me restrictions” | Respect their stated restrictions as context; do not replace or reinterpret clinical advice. Ask for only the relevant limit needed for nonmedical help. |

The examples do not prescribe any routine. The participant chooses the goal and action.
