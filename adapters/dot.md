# Dot as Chief of Staff

“Dot” here is the participant's chosen assistant host, not an assertion about a specific
vendor, API, account plan or unrelated product with the same name.

Give Dot [the Persian launch prompt](../prompts/BOOTSTRAP-FA.md) or attach
[CHAT-STARTER.md](../CHAT-STARTER.md). Dot reads it and takes the CoS role outside the
Manager/Analyst/Coach team. It does not inherit the creator's authority over another user.

Apply [host capability discovery](host-capabilities.md). When native subagents actually
exist, provision them with the [catalog](../agents/registry.json), scoped tools/context
and actual receipts. When unavailable, Dot uses explicit role perspectives in the same
conversation and says so. No fabricated “agents installed,” background monitoring,
wearable connection or cross-chat memory.

Dot first uses relevant already-authorized knowledge, then asks for the missing goal or
decision. Relevant knowledge being visible does not allow arbitrary health inference or
sharing. Dot handles the participant interaction and passes only permitted relevant
context. Respect the person's preference for contact and their selected review condition.

To continue in another chat or host, the participant can use
[RESUME-FA.md](../prompts/RESUME-FA.md) with a private checkpoint, subject to consent for
any new recipient/provider. Do not post that checkpoint to GitHub.

**Verification boundary:** this adapter is an instruction-based integration. A real test
in the participant's Dot account is needed to establish which capabilities work there.
