## Decisions

- Extend the established Python stack. Host inference, vision and transcription are used only when actually available; no additional model subscription is required by the runtime.
- SQLite outside Git is the MVP transactional primary store. The legacy CLI retains its JSON schema and commands. There is no implicit migration, local/cloud sync or active Sheets/Notion mirror.
- Manager owns organization, logging duties, report review and continuation. CoS owns the participant relationship, authority, external coordination and attention. The team returns ready packets/links.
- Activate only relevant responsibilities. A native worker requires creation and task receipts. Role names, catalog entries and service task claims do not prove independent execution.
- Begin with one participant's actual phone and a bounded selected history import, then incremental/replayed data. Missing, stale, corrected and unknown evidence remain visible. All-data scope is optional and explicit.
- HAE pricing evidence is dated research, not purchase authority: checked US seven-day trial, $6.99/year or $24.99 lifetime for Premium; verify the actual local purchase screen.
- OpenGym is optional. Use permitted app/page links; do not extract or forward its licensed media.
- Use ECC's separation of concerns and verification discipline, and V2's compact entry, bounded contracts and evidence-linked continuation. V2 remains a laboratory proposal. Do not import global hooks/settings, its delivery-role hierarchy or unsupported CAS/event-sourcing claims.

## Gates

1. **Composed code/package:** optional dependencies installed; full legacy/MVP tests, demo, loopback TCP/restart smoke, build and integrity validation pass with failures/skips recorded. Run [the CI helper](scripts/ci_mvp.py) or the commands in [the handoff](docs/mvp/CLI-HANDOFF.md).
2. **Reachability:** participant-approved always-on host, stable public HTTPS, private volume, supervised restart and tested backup/restore. Localhost is insufficient for phone connectivity. No public deployment or paid provisioning is performed by this artifact.
3. **Device:** selected iPhone Health permissions and HAE configuration; first real authorized upload, readback, replay/correction and measured delayed/background behavior. iOS timing is not guaranteed.
4. **Host/channel:** actual ChatGPT Dot delegation/tool access, provider exposure, bounded role execution and a real delivery receipt. Sending, reading, adoption, execution and outcome remain separate. Remote authenticated MCP/OAuth is a pending adapter.
5. **Participant:** purpose, data, recipients, retention, costs, goals and attention agreed; usefulness/burden reviewed. Silence is not agreement. No universal clinical thresholds or required daily reports.
